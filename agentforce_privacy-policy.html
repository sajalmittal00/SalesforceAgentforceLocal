Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cloudearly DWC-LLC ("Cloudearly", "we") handles information in SalesforceAgentforcelocal (the "App"), a chatbot that connects Microsoft Teams with Salesforce Agentforce.

1. Information processed

To answer user requests, the App may process messages entered in Teams and technical or account identifiers made available by Microsoft Teams. The content of a message may contain personal or business information that a user chooses to submit.

2. How information is used

We use the information to route requests to the configured Salesforce Agentforce service, retrieve responses, return them within Teams, and maintain security and operation of the integration.

3. Conversation storage and retention

The App does not maintain its own database or archive of chatbot conversations. Messages are processed to provide a response. This statement applies to storage controlled by the App itself; it does not mean that conversation content is never retained by Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, infrastructure providers, or the organization's own logging and compliance systems.

Any records kept by Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, or a connected customer organization are governed by their applicable contracts, configuration, privacy notices, and retention policies. Technical logs, if enabled by hosting or monitoring services, may be subject to separate retention settings.

4. Third-party services

The App communicates with Microsoft Teams and Salesforce Agentforce. Hosting or identity services may also process technical data required to operate the integration. These providers may process information under their respective agreements and applicable organizational settings.

5. Data protection

Access to App functionality is intended for authorized users and connected services. Communication with hosted services uses HTTPS where supported and required by the integration. Specific logging, access-control, encryption-at-rest, and monitoring configurations should be confirmed by Cloudearly before public launch.

6. Access and deletion requests

Because the App does not maintain a separate conversation archive, it does not provide an App-specific conversation-history deletion feature. For requests involving information stored in Teams, Salesforce, or the organization's systems, users should contact the administrator of their connected organization or the privacy contact below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and organizational policies.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, email navin@cloudearly.com (primary) or neha@cloudearly.com (alternate). You may also visit our Support page.

8. Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or its supporting services. The date above identifies the latest published revision.
