Support

Support for SalesforceAgentforcelocal, a Microsoft Teams chatbot connecting authorized users with Salesforce Agentforce.

Getting started

  1. Install the app in Microsoft Teams when it is available to your organization.
  2. Open the app from your Teams apps list.
  3. Complete any required organization-specific Salesforce access or sign-in steps.
  4. Send a message to the chatbot.

Troubleshooting

  • App cannot be installed: Contact your Teams administrator to check app permissions.
  • Authentication fails: Confirm that your organization has granted the necessary Salesforce access.
  • No reply or error: Restart the chat and contact support with the approximate time and error description. Avoid submitting passwords or sensitive customer data.
